A local charity I really like

🏡 Presse Purée: homesharing between generations

Over 60, with a spare room, and would like someone around the house? Since 2009, the Presse Purée association has matched seniors in Pau and the Béarn with young people under 30 (students, apprentices) looking for somewhere to live.

The young person pays a small rent and helps with a few everyday tasks (closing the shutters, taking out the bins, an errand). They are not a home help or a carer, but a reassuring presence, and often a lovely friendship. The association chooses the pairs and supports them throughout the year.

📍 Centre social la Pépinière, 6 avenue Robert Schuman, Pau · 📞 +33 6 83 51 66 92 (by appointment on Tuesdays and Fridays)
